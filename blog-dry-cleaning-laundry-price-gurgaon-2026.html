Why Do Retail Franchise Chains Charge 40% to 70% More?

Consumers frequently wonder why a 2-piece wool suit costs ₹450 at a Galleria Market or Sector 54 franchise outlet, while Mourya Dry Cleaners charges just ₹230 for the exact same German hydrocarbon solvent dry cleaning. The difference lies entirely in the business architecture:

  • Commercial Real Estate Overhead: Franchise stores rent 400–800 sq.ft retail spaces on expensive commercial strips (Galleria, Supermart, Baani Square, Rodeo Drive) where monthly rents range between ₹1.5 to ₹4 Lakhs. That markup is directly passed onto your per-piece bill.
  • Franchise Royalty & Software Fees: Franchisees must remit 8% to 12% of gross revenue to their corporate franchisor.
  • Outsourced Third-Party Processing: Many franchise retail counters do not clean garments on site. They bag items and dispatch them via courier to third-party industrial wash hubs outside Gurgaon, introducing delays and multi-tier contractor markups.
  • Direct Workshop Efficiency: Mourya Dry Cleaners operates its dedicated central cleaning facility at Dhobi Ghat, Sukhveer Singh Yadav Marg, near Educrest School in Basai Village, Sector 9B. By owning and operating our high-capacity commercial equipment, RO water softening plants, and fleet of delivery riders, we offer genuine factory-direct rates to Gurgaon residents.
