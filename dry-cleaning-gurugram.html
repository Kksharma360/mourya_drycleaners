Why choose dry cleaning over regular machine wash? +
Dry cleaning uses special non-water solvents that protect delicate fibers like wool, silk, rayon, and velvet from shrinking, fading, or losing shape. It dissolves stubborn oil and grease stains that normal detergent cannot lift.
How long does dry cleaning take? +
Our standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours. Express 24-hour service is available on request for urgent event wear.

How do you protect delicate beads and embroidery? +
Before cleaning, our master spotters inspect every embellishment and protect buttons/beadwork with custom netting or foil wrapping to prevent snagging or damage.
