Frequently Asked Questions — Commercial Laundry

What is the minimum order for commercial laundry? +
We typically work with businesses generating 20+ kg of laundry per day. However, we are flexible and can design custom plans for smaller operations like boutique salons, cafes, and startup offices.
Do you provide daily pickup for commercial clients? +
Yes! We offer daily fleet pickup and delivery schedules for hotels, PGs, hostels, gyms, and salons. Routes cover all Gurgaon sectors, with fixed morning pickup and evening delivery slots.
Can you provide GST billing for commercial laundry? +
Absolutely. All our commercial contracts include proper GST invoicing (GSTIN registered). Monthly billing cycles with detailed item/weight-wise breakdowns are standard.
What types of businesses do you serve? +
We serve hotels, guest houses, service apartments, PGs and hostels, salons and spas, gyms and fitness centers, restaurants and cafes, corporate offices, hospitals and clinics, and event/banquet halls.
How do you ensure hygiene for commercial clients? +
We use industrial-grade commercial washers with hot water sanitization cycles (60°C–90°C), hospital-grade disinfectants, and separate load processing for each client. No mixing of linens between businesses.
What are the commercial laundry rates? +
Commercial rates are customized based on volume, frequency, and item type. Typically 30–40% lower than retail per-piece rates. Contact us for a free quote tailored to your business needs.
