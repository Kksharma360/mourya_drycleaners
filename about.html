Our Story

Mourya Dry Cleaners was founded in 2025 from a small Dhobi Ghat workshop in Basai Village, Sector 9B, Gurugram. What began as a neighbourhood dry cleaning service trusted by local families has grown into one of Gurugram's most recommended fabric care specialists — serving over 191 sectors, colonies and villages.

Our founder's belief was simple: every garment deserves to be treated with the same care you put into choosing it. Whether it's a daily work shirt or a once-in-a-lifetime bridal lehenga, every item that comes through our doors is treated with that same standard.

Today, with free doorstep pickup and delivery across all Gurugram, we bring Gurugram-quality dry cleaning right to your home — without you ever having to step out.
