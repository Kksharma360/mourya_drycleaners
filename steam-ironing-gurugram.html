Saree Roll Polish (Charak) — Traditional Finishing for Silk Sarees

The Charak or Saree Roll Polish is a heritage Indian finishing technique prized by connoisseurs of fine silk sarees. Your Banarasi, Kanjeevaram, Chanderi, Tussar, or Patola silk saree is carefully starched with light rice starch, steam-pressed on a professional flat bed, and then rolled onto a smooth wooden cylinder — producing that distinctive crisp drape, sharp pleat lines, and lustrous sheen that makes silk sarees look brand new from the showroom.

This service is ideal for sarees stored for long periods, pre-wedding preparation, gifting, and post-function restoration. Available at our Sector 9B workshop with FREE doorstep pickup across Gurgaon.
