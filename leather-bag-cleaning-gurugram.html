Monsoon White Fungus Removal — Leather Bags & Jackets

Gurgaon's humid monsoon season (July–September) is the #1 enemy of leather goods. White fungal mold rapidly colonizes stored leather bags, jackets, shoes, and wallets — especially in poorly ventilated closets. Our 3-step anti-fungal treatment includes:

  • Step 1: Surface mold removal with dry brushing and vacuum extraction
  • Step 2: Deep anti-fungal sterilization with UV treatment and fungicidal solution
  • Step 3: Mink-oil conditioning and moisture-barrier application to prevent recurrence

Frequently Asked Questions — Leather & Bag Cleaning

How do you clean leather jackets without damaging them? +
We use specialized leather-safe solvents and hand-cleaning techniques that remove dirt, sweat, and stains without stripping natural oils. After cleaning, we apply mink-oil conditioning cream to restore suppleness and prevent cracking.
Can you remove white fungus from leather bags during monsoon? +
Yes! Monsoon fungus (white mold) on leather bags is one of our most common services. We use anti-fungal treatment, UV sterilization, and moisture-control conditioning to eliminate fungus and prevent recurrence.
Do you clean designer handbags like Gucci, Louis Vuitton, and Coach? +
Absolutely. We handle luxury designer bags with extreme care — hand-cleaning each panel, conditioning hardware, removing stains from lining, and restoring color and sheen. Coach, Michael Kors, Gucci, LV, Prada, Kate Spade, and more.
How much does leather jacket dry cleaning cost? +
Leather jacket cleaning starts at ₹280–₹450 depending on size and type (smooth leather, suede, nubuck). Price includes cleaning, conditioning, and minor color touch-up.
Can you clean suede coats and nubuck jackets? +
Yes. Suede and nubuck require specialized dry brushing, steam treatment, and nap revival techniques. We use dedicated suede erasers and brushes — never water or liquid solvents on nap surfaces.
Do you clean trolley bags and suitcases? +
Yes! We clean trolley bags, suitcases, duffel bags, laptop bags, and backpacks. Exterior cleaning, interior vacuum and sanitization, wheel and zipper lubrication, and stain removal are all included.
