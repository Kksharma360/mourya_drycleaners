Why You Should Never Wash Heavy Mink Blankets in Home Washing Machines

Most modern high-rise apartments in DLF Phase 5, Nirvana Country, or Sector 102 are equipped with 7kg to 9kg domestic front-loading or top-loading washing machines. While adequate for daily cotton shirts and jeans, putting a heavy 4kg double mink blanket into a home washer is a recipe for disaster:

  1. Weight Multiplication Under Water: Acrylic and polyester mink fibers are extremely absorbent. A dry 4.5kg blanket absorbs between 14kg to 18kg of water within 2 minutes of soaking. This massive weight exceeds the machine's motor torque limit, snapping drive belts or throwing drum suspension springs out of alignment.
  2. Fiber Matting & Loss of Softness: Standard laundry detergents contain optical brighteners and harsh builders that strip the siliconized coating from synthetic mink pile, leaving the blanket stiff, scratchy, and prone to static charge.
  3. Mold & Incomplete Drying: A soaked double blanket takes 3 to 5 days to air-dry during cloudy Gurgaon winter or spring days. This prolonged dampness breeds black mold inside inner quilting layers, producing an unpleasant mildew smell that is nearly impossible to eradicate.
