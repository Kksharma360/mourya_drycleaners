What is the minimum weight requirement for laundry pickup? +
Our minimum order for 100% free doorstep pickup and delivery is ₹350 (approx. 5–6 kg of laundry, or combined with dry cleaning items). If you have fewer clothes, you can combine with dry cleaning items or a nominal pickup fee applies.

Yes! Even inside your personal machine load, our laundry technicians separate whites from colored fabrics to avoid color bleeding.

You can place your laundry in any shopping bag or bucket. Our rider will transfer them into our branded laundry bags, weigh them, and provide a digital confirmation.
