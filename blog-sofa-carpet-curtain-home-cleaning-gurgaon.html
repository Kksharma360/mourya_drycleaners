Why Sofa "Dry Cleaning" is Often a Gimmick: Understanding Injection-Extraction

Many online service apps advertise "sofa dry cleaning" using dry foam sprays. While quick, dry foam only cleans the top 10% of the fabric surface, leaving dirt, body oils, and spilled milk rotting inside the inner polyurethane foam cushion.

Mourya uses true German injection-extraction shampooing technology:

  • Industrial HEPA Pre-Vacuuming: Dual-motor vacuum heads extract deep dry dust, pet hair, and food crumbs before liquids are introduced.
  • Enzyme Pre-Spotting: Specialized spotting solutions dissolve grease, tea, coffee, wine, and oil marks along armrests and headrests.
  • High-Pressure Solution Injection: Pressurized sanitizing liquid is shot deep into the upholstery weave to dissolve deeply trapped allergens.
  • Instant 95% Moisture Extraction: Heavy-duty vacuum suction claws immediately extract 95% of the dirty fluid into a waste tank. Your cushions dry within a swift 3 to 4 hours with no foul damp odor or mold risk.
